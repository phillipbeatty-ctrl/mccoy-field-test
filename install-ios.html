This guide is designed for Safari on an iPhone or iPad. Open this same address on that device to install Field Coach.

Put Field Coach on your Home Screen.

The iPhone and iPad web app installs directly from Safari. It remains the same organization-managed Field Coach account and does not require an App Store purchase.

Field Coach is already running as an installed Home Screen app on this device.
1
Open this page in Safari

Do not use an email preview, social-media browser, or Private Browsing window. Select “Open in Safari” first when necessary.

2
Open Safari’s Share menu

Tap the ⇧ Share button in Safari’s toolbar.

3
Select “Add to Home Screen”

Scroll through the actions if it is not immediately visible, then select Add to Home Screen.

4
Confirm the name and add it

Leave the name as Field Coach, then tap Add. The app icon will appear on the Home Screen.

5
Open, sign in, and allow field permissions

Open the new icon. Sign in with the assigned organization account, then allow location and camera access when Field Coach requests them.

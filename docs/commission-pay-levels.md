# 100% commission pay levels

McCoy assigns one commission pay level to each active seller. The assigned level is captured in the immutable compensation snapshot when a sale is submitted; changing a user's level does not silently rewrite prior sale snapshots.

| Product / speed | Trainee | Experienced Rep | Active Manager / Trainer |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Quantum | $150 | $200 | $250 |
| Brightspeed below 1 Gig | $150 | $100 | $150 |
| Brightspeed 1 Gig | $150 | $150 | $200 |
| Brightspeed 2+ Gig | $150 | $200 | $250 |
| AT&T Fiber below 1 Gig | $150 | $100 | $150 |
| AT&T Fiber 1+ Gig | $150 | $200 | $250 |

Additional configured amounts:

- AT&T Internet Air: $125 per sale.
- AT&T Mobile: $75 for the first line and $50 for each additional line.
- Over-the-phone mobile processor: $25 per qualifying line; this is excluded from the originating rep's estimate and remains an Accounting item.
- Weekly production increase: +$25 per qualifying sale at 15–24 weekly sales and +$50 per qualifying sale at 25+.
- Trainee Internet base protection: minimum $150 during the configured two-week trainee period.

Only ISP-verified eligible sales count toward weekly production tiers. OUT OF AREA / outside-system sales also require Admin approval. Final payment remains subject to install/activation, provider payment, and chargeback rules from the agreement.

DIRECTV and Vivint payouts remain intentionally unconfigured until the exact service-specific amounts are provided.

Admins assign pay levels in **Users & Managers**. Admin and authorized Accounting users can view the pay-scale matrix in **Compensation Controls**. Reps see only their own assigned pay level, weekly progress, and estimated qualifying commission.
